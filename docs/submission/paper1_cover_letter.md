# Cover Letter — Paper 1

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript **“Simplex Cubic Decomposition over 2-Power Rings: Global Identifiability from Binary Contractions and Exact Bit Lifting”** for publication in *Linear Algebra and its Applications*.

The paper proves a constructive identifiability theorem for an overcomplete family of symmetric cubic tensors over the finite local rings Z/(2^m). For even n >= 4, an (n+1)-term decomposition is globally identifiable when the parity reductions of its atoms form a binary simplex frame. The proof first recovers the parity frame from full-rank tensor contractions over F_2 and then proves that every successive 2-adic lift is unique because the one-bit cubic Jacobian is injective.

The result is overcomplete already at r=n+1 and is stated independently of its motivating computational framework. The manuscript also proves a structured residual-simplex r=n+2 extension: the extra parity atom is exposed by the tensor diagonal, and the lift is unique under an explicit support/complement condition. The Z_256, n=8 case is accompanied by an exact integer verifier.

The paper is distinct from generic real or complex overcomplete decomposition results and from finite-field tensor-rank work: the ground object here is the non-field local ring Z/(2^m), and the recovery mechanism couples binary contraction geometry with exact bit lifting.

An author preprint and reference verification code are publicly available in the project repository. This manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

Muhammad Arshad
