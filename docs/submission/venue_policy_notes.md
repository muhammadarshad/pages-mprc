# Venue and Preprint Policy Notes

Audit date: 18 September 2026

## Paper 1

### Primary: Linear Algebra and its Applications

The journal states that it publishes new information or insights in matrix theory and finite-dimensional linear algebra, including algebraic, arithmetic, combinatorial and geometric aspects.

Fit:
- symmetric tensors and finite-dimensional multilinear algebra;
- arithmetic over finite local rings;
- exact identifiability/recovery theorem.

### Secondary: Linear and Multilinear Algebra

Its stated scope includes spaces over fields or rings, tensor algebras, Lie theory and operator theory.

## Paper 2

### Primary: Linear Algebra and its Applications

Fit:
- module linear independence;
- combinatorial/arithmetic matrix structure;
- explicit invertible incidence minor.

### Secondary: SIAM Journal on Discrete Mathematics

Its scope includes algebraic/enumerative combinatorics and combinatorial matrix theory. This becomes the stronger fit if the manuscript is reframed around the Boolean support/incidence structure rather than tensor applications.

## Paper 3

### Primary: Linear and Multilinear Algebra

Fit:
- spaces over rings;
- operator theory;
- tensor algebra;
- Lie/commutator structure.

### Stretch: Journal of Algebra

Journal of Algebra asks for substantial algebraic results with lasting impact. Submission there should wait until the ring-theoretic statements are generalized beyond the n=8 specialization.

## Public preprint status

The MPRC booklet/GitHub manuscripts are author preprints.

Elsevier's general sharing policy states that authors may share preprints anywhere at any time and that preprints do not count as prior publication under its general policy, subject to journal-specific exceptions.

Taylor & Francis likewise states that posting an Author's Original Manuscript on a non-commercial preprint server is not duplicate publication and does not prevent later submission.

Before actual submission:
1. re-check the target journal's own Guide for Authors;
2. disclose the public preprint in the cover letter/submission form;
3. cite or link the preprint if the target specifically requests it;
4. after acceptance, update the public preprint with the Version of Record DOI.
