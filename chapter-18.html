on Z₂₅₆

Half-turn reversal, exact Q₈ embedding, CW/CCW orientation, and 720° state closure · September 2026

Abstract

Quaternion Sign as a Ring Half-Turn

This chapter studies Q₈ on the MPRC quarter-lattice {0,64,128,192} ⊂ Z₂₅₆. Hamilton multiplication is the declared external starting algebra. The embedding is exact: quaternion orientation reversal is represented by the MPRC half-turn R₁₂₈, preserving the resulting axis while shifting its ring position by exactly 128. The same construction gives I²=J²=K²=IJK=128 and separates 360° rotational closure from 720° full-state closure.

Claim boundary. The Q₈→Z₂₅₆ embedding and its half-turn/double-cover properties are proved. The stronger claim that MPRC CW/CCW geometry alone generates Hamilton anti-commutation remains an open conjecture.

01 · Starting Algebra

Hamilton Is the Input

i² = j² = k² = ijk = −1
ij=k, jk=i, ki=j
ji=−k, kj=−i, ik=−j

The cyclic products preserve orientation; reversing the traversal changes only the orientation of the resulting axis.

02 · Quarter-Lattice Embedding

Q₈ on {0,64,128,192}

χ(a,0)=1
χ(a,64)=a
χ(a,128)=−1
χ(a,192)=−a

No Euler formula, trigonometric evaluation, or floating-point phase is required.

03 · Identity

MPRC Half-Turn Orientation Identity

χ(a,s+128 mod 256)=−χ(a,s)

Verified for every a∈{I,J,K} and s∈{0,64,128,192}. Thus R₁₂₈:s↦s+128 is exactly quaternion orientation reversal inside this embedding.
