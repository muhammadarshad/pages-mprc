# Cover Letter — Paper 2

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript **“Exact Free Capacity of Symmetric Rank-One Cubic Dictionaries over Dyadic Rings”** for publication in *Linear Algebra and its Applications*.

The manuscript determines exactly how many symmetric rank-one cubic atoms can form a free linearly independent dictionary over the finite local ring Z/(2^m). The answer is

Cap_3(n,m) = sum_{k=1}^{min(3,n)} binom(n,k).

The upper bound is forced by the characteristic-two shadow: repeated powers collapse and every cubic atom reduces to square-free supports of sizes one, two or three. Any larger family therefore has a binary dependence, which lifts to an exact nonzero module relation after multiplication by 2^(m-1). A matching lower bound is obtained from an explicit dense family whose selected cubic minor reduces to the unitriangular zeta matrix of the truncated Boolean lattice.

For n=8 the exact capacity is 92. The manuscript carefully distinguishes this free-dictionary capacity from generic tensor rank and from unknown-atom identifiability. The subset-incidence ingredient is treated as classical; the contribution is the sharp dyadic-ring capacity theorem and its explicit attaining family.

An author preprint and pure-integer verifier are publicly available in the project repository. This manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

Muhammad Arshad
