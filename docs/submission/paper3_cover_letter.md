# Cover Letter — Paper 3

Dear Editor,

Please consider the manuscript **“An Eight-State Operator and Transpose Geometry over Dyadic Rings: Trace-Zero Modules, Mixed-Radix Addressing, and Tensor-Lift Kernels”** for publication in *Linear and Multilinear Algebra*.

The paper develops an exact finite operator geometry over R_m=Z/(2^m). In an eight-state labeling, the trace-zero submodule of M_8(R_m) has a free rank-63 basis. Fourteen reference couplings generate every trace-zero basis direction by module span and elementary commutators. A seven-state orientation yields the classical Frobenius group F_21; this classical component is explicitly attributed rather than presented as new.

The manuscript then connects the operator algebra to a frozen mixed-radix address with coefficients 64,21,7,1 and proves an involutive transpose orbit law. Its final result is a general tensor-lift kernel theorem:

ker rho_l = {lambda I_8 : l lambda = 0 in R_m}.

For Z_256 this gives a two-element central kernel at order two, a faithful order-three action, and a four-element kernel {0,64I,128I,192I} at order four.

The paper is positioned as a synthesis of matrix algebra over rings, finite operator geometry and tensor actions. It does not claim novelty for F_21, nor does it identify the construction with G_2, octonions or so(8).

An author preprint and exact verifier are publicly available in the project repository. This manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,

Muhammad Arshad
