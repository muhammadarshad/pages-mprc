The Accumulation-Differential Identity
Exact N-Dimensional Recovery from Two Navigation Observables

Muhammad Arshad · 2026 · Exact recovery, minimality, ring-native form, and Identity-2 wave trace evaluation

Abstract

Two Observables, Full Path Recovery

The Accumulation-Differential Identity (ADI) is an exact recovery theorem for a structured family of sequential navigation vectors. For an N-dimensional path, the observable pair is the total accumulation Λ and the opening differential δ₁=a₁−a₂. The remaining differentials are generated by the odd-increment rule δ_k=δ₁+(k²−1). The full vector is then recovered in O(N) operations. The accompanying manuscript reports 400,500 random trials from N=2 through N=256 with zero reconstruction errors. fileciteturn40file0L49-L64

Scope boundary. ADI is not a general compression theorem for arbitrary vectors. Its exactness applies to the stated sequential-navigation family satisfying the odd-increment constraint in the L¹ coordinate-sum geometry. fileciteturn40file0L684-L690

01 · Encoding

The ADI Pair

For (a₁,…,a_N), define:

Λ = Σᵢ aᵢ
δ₁ = a₁ − a₂

The first quantity carries the total accumulated path. The second fixes the opening coordinate difference. fileciteturn40file0L273-L279

02 · Odd-Increment Rule

All Later Differentials Are Derived

δ_k = δ₁ + (k² − 1),   k=2,…,N−1
k²−1 = 3+5+⋯+(2k−1)

For δ₁=11 the differential sequence begins 11,14,19,26,35,… with successive gaps 3,5,7,9,… . fileciteturn40file0L281-L299

The geometric construction interprets each added dimension as a new rectangular L¹ layer. Since k²−(k−1)²=2k−1, each successive layer contributes the next odd increment. fileciteturn40file0L347-L363

03 · Recovery Theorem

Exact Reconstruction of the Whole Vector

a₁ = (Λ + Σ_{k=1}^{N−1} δ_k) / N
a_{k+1} = a₁ − δ_k

Because a_{k+1}=a₁−δ_k, summing all coordinates gives Λ=Na₁−Σδ_k. Solving for a₁ yields the first equation, and every remaining coordinate follows by subtraction. fileciteturn40file0L301-L320

04 · Minimality Theorem

Why Exactly Two Values

Within the ADI family, Λ and δ₁ are both necessary and sufficient. Without Λ, the total scale is undetermined. Without δ₁, the differential chain cannot be generated. No third independent observable is required because all later δ_k are deterministic consequences of δ₁. fileciteturn40file0L433-L459

minimal encoding = {Λ, δ₁}

06 · Complexity

Recovery Cost Is O(N)

ADI recovery needs one division and N−1 subtractions. The manuscript contrasts this with a general N×N linear-system solve at O(N³). At N=256 it reports 256 identity operations versus 16,777,216 general-solver operations, an operation-count ratio of 65,536×, with the reported wall-clock comparison approximately 104× on the tested implementation. fileciteturn40file0L545-L560

07 · Ring-Native Form

ADI on Z₂₅₆

Λ = (Σᵢ aᵢ) mod 256
δ₁ = (a₁−a₂) mod 256
δ_k = (δ₁+k²−1) mod 256

For N=2^k, the manuscript proposes a right-shift implementation for the division step; for odd N, modular inversion is available whenever gcd(N,256)=1. fileciteturn40file0L611-L660

The ring-native form is a specialised implementation of the same recovery identity, not a claim that every arbitrary Z₂₅₆ vector is compressible into two numbers.

10 · Performance Claim Boundary

Streaming Advantage, Not Universal FFT Dominance

The twenty-thread ratios must be read carefully: the source attributes much of the enlarged advantage to FFTW thread-launch overhead at the tested small transform sizes, not to a new ADI capability. It also notes that raw FFT snapshot latency can still be lower than the current Identity-2 encode path at larger dimensions. The defensible claim is exact inverse recoverability and wave-native streaming efficiency, not universal superiority over every standalone FFT workload. fileciteturn40file1L760-L760
